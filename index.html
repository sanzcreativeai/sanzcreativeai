Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does SanzCreative AI offer?

We're a Chennai-based AI digital marketing agency offering website development, SEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads management, social media marketing, AI poster design, video and Reels editing, and branding/logo design — all under one roof.

Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?

Yes. While we're based in Kolathur, Chennai, we work with businesses, startups, and freelancers remotely across India — everything from strategy calls to project delivery happens online.

How much does a website or poster design cost?

Project pricing starts from ₹399 for a poster or social creative, ₹499 for an Instagram Reel edit, ₹599 for a logo, and ₹499+ for website development depending on scope. See our Pricing section for full details.

Do you offer Google Ads and social media marketing management?

Yes — we manage Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns alongside ongoing social media marketing and management for businesses that want consistent, hands-off growth.

How long does a typical project take?

A poster or Reel edit is usually delivered within a few days; a full website build typically takes 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. We'll always give you a clear timeline before starting.

How do I get a custom quote?

Fill out the form in our Contact section with what you need — we reply the same day with a clear quote based on your project's scope.
