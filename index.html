Frequently Asked Questions

What does SanzCreative.ai actually do?

We're an AI-powered creative studio based in Chennai building premium websites, brand identity, AI posters, video editing, Google Ads and Meta Ads management, and social media marketing — all under one roof.

Do you work with clients outside Chennai?

Yes. We're based in Kolathur, Chennai, and work with businesses and startups remotely across India from strategy through delivery.

How much does a project cost?

Project pricing starts from ₹399 for a poster or social creative, ₹499 for a reel edit, ₹599 for a logo, and ₹499+ for website development depending on scope. See our Pricing section.

Do you manage Google Ads and social media?

Yes — we manage Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns alongside ongoing social media marketing for businesses that want consistent, hands-off growth.

How long does a project take?

A poster or reel edit is usually delivered within a few days; a full website build typically takes 1-2 weeks depending on complexity.

How do I get a custom quote?

Fill out the form in our Contact section with what you need — we reply the same day with a clear quote based on your project's scope.
